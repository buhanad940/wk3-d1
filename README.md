# NYUAD Compass 🧭

A big, self-contained student guide and four-year planner for **New York University Abu Dhabi**, built around an
Economics major on the Rhodes Scholarship track, with study-away semesters in Florence (sophomore spring) and
New York (junior fall).

## Run it

No build step, no server, no dependencies. Either:

- Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or
- Serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`) and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

All personal data (checklist progress, professor comparison entries) is stored only in your browser's
localStorage — nothing leaves your machine.

## Important disclaimer

This is an **unofficial planning aid**, not an NYUAD or NYU publication. Course names, codes, professor
assignments, meeting times, requirements, and policies change every semester — everything consequential must be
confirmed through the official course search (Albert), academic advisers, and the relevant NYUAD offices.
The app repeats this in every section on purpose.
